JOB TITLE: Cybersecurity Analyst

ROLE OVERVIEW
We are seeking a junior Cybersecurity Analyst to support security monitoring, vulnerability assessment, network analysis, and incident documentation. The role follows approved procedures and focuses on identifying, documenting, and escalating potential security concerns.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Review security alerts and assist with initial incident triage.
- Analyze system and network logs to identify unusual activity and potential indicators of compromise.
- Use network analysis tools such as Wireshark to inspect authorized traffic captures.
- Assist with vulnerability scanning, assessment reports, and remediation tracking.
- Apply foundational knowledge of TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP/S, firewalls, and common web security risks.
- Document findings clearly and escalate incidents according to established playbooks.
- Support security awareness, asset documentation, and repeatable operational procedures.

REQUIRED SKILLS
- Foundational knowledge of cybersecurity and network security.
- Understanding of TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP/S, and firewall concepts.
- Familiarity with Linux and Windows environments.
- Basic knowledge of vulnerability assessment and incident response.
- Ability to analyze logs and document findings.
- Scripting familiarity with Python or Bash is beneficial.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
- Exposure to Wireshark, Nmap, Burp Suite Community, Splunk, or SIEM platforms.
- Familiarity with OWASP Top 10 and common web application vulnerabilities.
- Home-lab, coursework, internship, or authorized practice-lab experience.
- Strong attention to detail, risk awareness, and communication skills.
